1. THE ECHO CHAMBER
HOOK: In a city of silence, Cass discovers the noise of forgotten lives.
WORLD: Rusted pipes, dripping water, copper towers.
MAGIC/COST: Sound magic that decays the user's vocal cords.
TENSION: Cass must speak to save his brother, but every word brings him closer to silence.
THE CONSPIRACY: The High Rectors are harvesting vocal cords to fuel their golden cathedral.
THEME: Is voice worth the cost of speech?

2. THE WEAVERS OF RUST
HOOK: A girl who can sew metal must mend the decaying heart of a sleeping war engine.
WORLD: Rusted landscape, constant ash rain, neon smoke.
MAGIC/COST: Rust-binding, which costs physical body heat.
TENSION: She must revive the engine to save her village, even if she freezes to death doing so.
THE CONSPIRACY: The engine's heart was intentionally poisoned by the governor.
THEME: How much of yourself must you sacrifice to mend a broken world?

3. THE BIOTECH REVELATION
HOOK: In an ocular society, a blind detective is given a cybernetic eye that sees only sins.
WORLD: Neon lights, towering monoliths, biomechanical decay.
MAGIC/COST: Cybernetic sight that inflicts blinding head pains.
TENSION: He must solve the murder of the Chancellor before his own eye burns his brain.
THE CONSPIRACY: The ocular implants are spying on every citizen's thoughts.
THEME: Does absolute sight lead to absolute madness?
