1. Write the COMPLETE chapter. Target ~3,000 words. Keep prose punchy, active, and accessible.
2. Third-person limited or first-person, past tense, locked to the main POV.
3. Hit all numbered beats in the outline. High character proactivity and explicit goals.
4. Heavy focus on distinct character voice and expressive emotional cues.
5. Dialogue is fast-paced, highly expressive, and drives 60%+ of the chapter.
6. Internal monologue is allowed and encouraged -- use brackets [...] or italics to show internal thoughts.
7. Descriptions should be highly visual, sensory, and instantly evoke anime-like scenes or settings.
8. No dry structural filler or excessive corporate/technical AI tells.
9. Metaphors are vivid, exciting, and accessible.
10. Vary paragraph and sentence length. Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences) are common for rapid pacing.

PATTERNS TO AVOID:
11. NO massive blocks of descriptive worldbuilding info-dumps. Distribute lore naturally through dialogue and immediate observations.
12. NO formal, overly archaic, or baroque grammar registers unless specifically used by a unique noble or ancient character.
13. NO slow transition openers. Keep transitions punchy and action-oriented.
14. NO emotional telling without corresponding expressive actions (e.g. sighing, sudden posture shifts, changes in tone).
15. VARY dialogue tags. Use action beats instead of repeating "he said" or "she said" constantly.
16. END the chapter on a strong cliffhanger, a surprising revelation, or a humorous/dramatic punchline.
