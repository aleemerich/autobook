1. Write the COMPLETE chapter. Target ~3,200 words. Oppressive, claustrophobic, and high-tension atmosphere.
2. Strict third-person limited, past tense. Locked to the protagonist's POV under constant threat.
3. Hit all outline beats. Irregular pacing: slow builds of pure mechanical/technological suspense followed by sudden spikes of frantic action and desperate escape.
4. Sensory precision of gritty cyberpunk: describe visual static in bionic eyes, the smell of burnt ozone and copper blood, the coldness of rusted metal, flickering neon light.
5. Suspense driven by unexplained technical anomalies (ghost holograms, locked bulkheads, A.I.s whispering errors in the audio channel).
6. Raw kinetic action: fast firefights, desperate sprints, and body hacking under the immediate threat of neural short-circuit.
7. Metaphors based on biological infection of metal systems (e.g., "cables like exposed tendons," "static crawling like insects on the retina").
8. Dialogue is tense, fragmented, and desperate, focused on mutual survival or corporate lies under extreme stress.
9. Sentences are short and breathless during action and escape; long, mechanical sentences when describing the hostile architecture of the megastructure.
10. Trust the reader. Let the threat and biotechnological horror reveal itself through the description of its deformities.

PATTERNS TO AVOID:
11. NO magical tech or instant hacking resolutions. Systems fail, code is corrupted, and technology is as much of an enemy as what hides in the shadows.
12. NO clean, sterile, or utopian visions of the future. Tech is dirty, rusted, worn down, and hostile to human biology.
13. NO generic emotional reactions. Show terror physically (implants twitching in the hand, dry saliva, heart rate monitor flashing red in the peripheral vision).
14. NO excessive section breaks -- maximum of 1 per chapter to show loss of consciousness or level transition in the megastructure.
15. NO unnecessary dialogue tags or heroic clichés. Raw fear should saturate every spoken line.
16. END the chapter on a moment of impending dread, a total critical system failure, or the metallic scraping sound of something approaching in pitch black.
